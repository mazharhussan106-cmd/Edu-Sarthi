# Premium Vocabulary Flashcard Master Specification

## Goal

Create a consistent deck of 1,000 premium English vocabulary flashcards.

## Page Layout

-   Size: **A3 Landscape (420 × 297 mm)**
-   Split vertically 50% / 50%
-   **Left:** Front (Recognition)
-   **Right:** Back (Learning & Usage)
-   Add center fold/cut guide.
-   Large margins and print-safe spacing.

## Front (Recognition)

-   Illustration
-   Word
-   IPA
-   Syllable break (stress highlighted)
-   Indian pronunciation
-   English sound-out
-   Part of speech
-   Grammar category
-   CEFR
-   Frequency
-   Register
-   Tone
-   Word origin
-   Pronunciation tip
-   Spelling tip
-   Quick memory hint

### Verb cards

Include a 3-step action sequence: 1. Before 2. During 3. After

## Back (Learning & Usage)

-   Simple meaning
-   Hindi meaning
-   Plain explanation
-   Verb forms (if applicable)
-   Grammar patterns
-   Action timeline
-   Collocations
-   Example sentences
-   Conversation
-   Common mistakes
-   Compare/confusing words
-   Related words
-   Memory trick
-   Practice zone
-   Review schedule (Day 1, 3, 7, 15, 30)

## Pronunciation

Always include: - IPA - Syllable breakdown - Stressed syllable
highlighted - Indian pronunciation (Hindi) - English sound-out

## Design

-   Premium infographic style
-   Consistent layout
-   Large readable fonts
-   Vector-style illustrations
-   Icons for sections
-   White space for readability

## Purpose

Optimized for: - Printing - Digital flashcards - Active recall -
Speaking - Long-term retention
