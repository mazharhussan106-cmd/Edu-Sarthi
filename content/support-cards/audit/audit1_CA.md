## Summary

141 logged changes across all 20 cards; the audit now reports no error for this file or its IDs (only the 160 expected "card missing" lines for other groups).

By type:
- MCQ (33): all 20 sets of options rebalanced so the correct option is no longer a giveaway (the audit had flagged 20 of 20; now 0 are clearly the longest, answer letters unchanged), plus 9 mcq_why and 2 mcq_question rewrites to match, including removing Indian-English phrases used as wrong answers.
- Fill-the-blank (31 rows): 15 blanks had more than one defensible answer (aim/strive, continue/help, summary/note, digits/numbers, by/at, loan/replacement and so on), so the blank was moved to a word that fits only once.
- Reorder (10 rows): 2 jumbles were below 5 words (CSC-A-010, CSC-A-017), 1 had two valid answers (CSC-A-008), 2 now mark the sentence start with a capital.
- Pronunciation fields (9): wrong claim that "service starts with v", stress line missing words (CSC-A-003, CSC-A-010), "whatever" stress contradicting its own tip, linking contradicting the tip (CSC-A-007), jargon "retroflex", a non-trap "no ch in syncing".
- Honesty and safety (about 14): removed promises an agent cannot keep (a fix "so it doesn't happen again", an invented cause and incident report, "you'll hear from us by noon", "the team lead reads every one", an unchecked "keeps all your files"), softened refund timing to "should reach", and an admission before facts were checked.
- Rules and empathy: added regret to the refusal in CSC-A-002 (reason + empathy + alternative), made the refund context explicit in CSC-A-003, marked an unsourced boundary rule as derived advice and attributed HEARD.
- Cultural notes (6): replaced unsupported claims about "Indian agents" with phrasing switches or neutral second-language points; Indian-English phrases (as per policy, concerned team, at the earliest, not in our scope, kindly, from our side only) moved out of watch_out and wrong MCQ options.
- Hindi (6 rows): dropped the "full" and "already" mismatches, added the new apology line; Roman Hindi kept in step.
- Duplicates and small fixes: near-identical customer lines in whats_next vs if_customer_pushes (CSC-A-015, 017, 019), ask_next repeating whats_next_reply (CSC-A-010), ask_next promising an update before the new address was known (CSC-A-007).

Not resolved: nothing blocking. Two judgement calls for the coordinator: CSC-A-002's sentence grew to 30 words (the chat warning threshold is above 30), and the placeholder "[number]" in CSC-A-014 was left because the card needs a slot, not a real phone number.
