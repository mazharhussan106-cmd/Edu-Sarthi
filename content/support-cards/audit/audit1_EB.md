## Summary

All 20 cards (CSE-B-001 to CSE-B-020) were reviewed field by field and 158 changes were logged across every card. By type: accuracy and honesty 47 (generic apologies made specific, empathy added before action, firm promises about couriers and banks softened to "due to" or "should", invented usage claims removed, weekday/date clashes removed); MCQ 39 (every correct option was clearly the longest, so all 20 option sets were rebuilt with full, similar-length, plausible distractors and all 19 mcq_why lines rewritten to match; the answer letters are unchanged); practice 29 (fill_blank items that had two or more valid answers were moved to a blank with one answer, reorder jumbles of 4 words were extended to 5 or more, prompts and model answers made to match, duplicated follow-up lines replaced); pronunciation 23 (noun stress REE-fund, EE-mail, WRY-ting, DAN-yel, spelled-out letters such as pee-EM and oh-ar-DEE, missing v/w and f/p mouth instructions with minimal pairs); Hindi 7 (Devanagari and Roman lines aligned, phone idiom removed from an email card, clumsy word order and "खराब" for "damaged" corrected); English 7; duplicates 5; level 1 ("phishing" replaced for A2). One safety fix matters most: CSE-B-012 taught asking customers to email their bank account details, so the whole scenario now asks the customer to choose card or store credit instead (6 fields changed). The audit reports no error for this file or its IDs; the only remaining lines are the 160 expected "card missing" lines for other groups.

Left as is, on purpose: the fill_blank on CSE-B-011 ("Best ____" could also be "wishes") and on CSE-B-017 ("needful" could also be "necessary") each have a small chance of a second valid answer that I could not remove without making the exercise artificial or trivial.
