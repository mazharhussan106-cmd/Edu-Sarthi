## Summary

All 20 Email · Intermediate cards were reviewed field by field and fixed in four batches of five, plus one final tidy (CSE-I-011 watch_out). The log holds 128 rows. Each row can cover more than one field of a card, for example the Devanagari line and its Roman line, or the four options of one MCQ. Rows by type: 45 English, accuracy and honesty fixes in the teaching text (over-promises such as "approve your claim" or "arrange a replacement", invented company rules, unsourced "Indian emails often…" claims, contradictions between fields, the Indian-English phrases "revert", "kindly", "at the earliest" and "under process" removed from agent lines, distractors and ✗ lines and kept only in cultural_note, dont_say and positive_swap); 34 MCQ rewrites (options of similar length and equal plausibility, mcq_why kept consistent; answer letters and option order untouched; the audit's "correct option clearly longest" error is gone for this file); 18 pronunciation fixes (a linking note that quoted words not in the sentence, a wrong /s/ to /z/ respelling, stress lines that stopped early or dropped "the" and "of", falls on mid-sentence words, weak Indian-speaker tips replaced with sounds that are really in the sentence); 11 fill_blank changes so each has one defensible answer; 10 other practice fixes (whats_next replies that repeated if_customer_pushes, "pin code" and "moved back" in model translations); 9 Hindi fixes (Devanagari and Roman now match word for word, plus meaning slips such as "over two weeks" and "I trust"); 1 reorder fix (CSE-I-016 had only 4 words, which was the one audit ERROR for my file, now 8). No id, channel, level, kind, call_stage, speaker, cefr, register, importance or mcq_answer value was changed. Final audit for cards_EI.txt: 0 errors and 0 warnings (the 160 "card missing" lines belong to other groups). Open point: weekday-plus-date pairs such as "Thursday, 18 April" (CSE-I-006) are true only in certain years. I removed the one stray case in CSE-I-003 but left CSE-I-006, where the date runs through about 15 fields; a calendar-neutral date would need one coordinated edit.
