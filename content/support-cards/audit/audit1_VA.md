Only the sentences of 003 and 012 changed; every field that quotes them was updated and a search finds no stale copy. Left as written on purpose: the comma in "Please go on, I'm listening." (idiomatic speech) and "I'll sort out your booking" in 004 (a conditional ownership promise, not an unkeepable one). Nothing is unresolved. Final audit: no ERROR or warning mentions cards_VA.txt; the only errors left are the 160 "card missing" lines for the other groups.
