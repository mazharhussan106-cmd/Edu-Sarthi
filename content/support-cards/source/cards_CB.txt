stress: HI · thanks-for-con-TACT-ing-[COM-pa-ny] · i'm-a-NAN-ya · how-can-i-HELP-you-to-DAY
linking: If read aloud: thanks_for → "thanks-fer" · help_you → "hel-pyoo" · I'm_Ananya → "ai-ma-NAN-ya" (the "m" joins the next vowel)
intonation: If read aloud: a friendly lift on "Hi", a short pause after the company name, and a falling tone on "How can I help you today?" because it is a wh- question.
indian_pron_tip: If read aloud: "thanks" starts with a soft "th" (tongue lightly between the teeth), not a hard "t" as in "tanks". Keep the final "s".
register: Friendly
tone: Warm, welcoming, ready to help
importance: 5
customer_emotion: Hopeful but unsure whether a real person is on the other side.
memory_hook: Think of a shop door bell: hello, the shop's name, your name tag and "how can I help?", all in one ring.
hindi_meaning: नमस्ते, [Company] से संपर्क करने के लिए धन्यवाद। मैं अनन्या हूँ। आज मैं आपकी कैसे मदद कर सकता/सकती हूँ?
hindi_roman: Namaste, [Company] se sampark karne ke liye dhanyavaad. Main Ananya hoon. Aaj main aapki kaise madad kar sakta/sakti hoon?
simple_meaning: Hello, thank you for writing to us. My name is Ananya. What do you need help with?
why_it_works: It tells the customer a real person has joined, gives a name they can use, and ends with an open question so they can explain the problem in their own words.
key_chunk: Hi, thanks for contacting ___. I'm ___.
grammar_pattern: Thanks + for + verb-ing · How + can + I + base verb + you …?
grammar_note: After "thanks for", use the -ing form: "thanks for contacting", not "thanks for contact".
tone_note: "Hi" and "thanks" sound human and friendly. "Dear Customer, state your query" sounds like a robot and makes the customer write back coldly.
watch_out: ✗ Hi, thanks for contact [Company]. How I can help you? → ✓ Hi, thanks for contacting [Company]. How can I help you today?
dont_say: "Hello Dear Customer, please state your query." — it is cold, sounds automated and gives no name.
positive_swap: Instead of "State your query", say "How can I help you today?"
alt_1: Hello and welcome to [Company]. My name's Ananya. What can I help you with?
alt_2: Hi there, you're chatting with Ananya from [Company]. How can I help?
more_formal: Good morning, and thank you for contacting [Company]. My name is Ananya. How may I help you today?
ask_next: Thanks, Sarah. Could you share your order number so I can take a look?
replies_you_may_hear: Hi, my parcel hasn't arrived. · I want to change my delivery address. · is this a bot?
if_customer_pushes: Customer: "Is this a bot?" → Agent: "No, I'm Ananya, a real person on the [Company] support team. How can I help you today?"
cultural_note: Many Indian agents introduce themselves with "Myself Ananya"; international customers may not expect this, so switch to "I'm Ananya" or "My name's Ananya" in chat.
channel_note: Voice: "Thank you for calling [Company]. This is Ananya." Email: "Hi Sarah, thanks for getting in touch," with your name in the sign-off.
when_not_to_use: Do not send the full greeting if a colleague has already greeted the customer and passed the chat to you; write "Hi Sarah, I'm Ananya from billing" instead.
mini_conversation: Customer: hello | Agent: Hi, thanks for contacting [Company]. I'm Ananya. How can I help you today? | Customer: my parcel is late | Agent: I'm sorry it's late. Could you share your order number so I can check?
source_rule: Chat: greet with your name, as you would on a call, and ask an open question.

Please state your query so we can help. | C) Hi, thanks for contacting [Company]. I'm Ananya. How can I help you today? | D) HELLO!! WELCOME TO [Company]!! HOW CAN I HELP YOU TODAY?? 😊
