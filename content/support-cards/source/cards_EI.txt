indian_pron_tip: If read aloud: "without" starts with rounded lips for "w" (not teeth on lip, "vithout"), and its "th" is soft with the tongue between the teeth, not a hard "d".

simple_meaning: Thanks for writing, David. Your order came without the charger, and you want us to send it quickly. I understand the problem.
why_it_works: Restating the problem in one sentence proves you read the whole email and lets the customer correct you before you act. It also prevents a second email that starts "As I already said…".
key_chunk: I understand that ___, and you'd like ___.
grammar_pattern: I understand that + past simple (what happened) + and you'd like + object + past participle
grammar_note: "Arrived" is past simple for what happened; "you'd like" is a polite way to say what the customer wants.
tone_note: Naming his exact problem sounds attentive. Starting with "We have received your query regarding the above" sounds like a machine and tells him nothing.
watch_out: ✗ I understood your problem that charger is not came. → ✓ I understand that your order arrived without the charger.
dont_say: "Your query has been noted." It is cold, says nothing about his problem and gives no sign that anyone read the email.
positive_swap: Instead of "We have received your complaint regarding the above order", say "I understand that your order 52817 arrived without the charger."
alt_1: Hi David, thanks for getting in touch. I'm sorry to hear that the charger was missing from order 52817, and I can see you need it quickly.
alt_2: Hi David, thank you for writing to us. If I've understood correctly, your laptop arrived but the charger was not in the box.
more_formal: Dear Mr Hughes, thank you for your email. I understand that order 52817 was delivered without its charger and that you would like the missing item sent urgently.
ask_next: I've checked your order and arranged for a charger to be sent to you today by express delivery, and you will receive a tracking link by email this evening.

channel_note: Voice: "So, if I've understood correctly, the charger was missing. Is that right?" Chat: "I can see your charger was missing from order 52817."
when_not_to_use: Do not restate a long, sensitive complaint word for word; summarise it in one neutral sentence instead.
