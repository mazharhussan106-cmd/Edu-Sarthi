Build se pehle ye clear karo

Platform badal raha hai

Spec kehti hai student app native Kotlin/iOS hoga. Aaj ka code Next.js website hai. Ya to student side naya banega, ya pehle PWA/web me chalega — decide karo.

Phone OTP aur SendGrid

Spec me +91 phone OTP "SendGrid" se hai. SendGrid email bhejta hai, SMS nahi. Phone OTP ke liye MSG91 / Twilio jaisa SMS provider chahiye.

Teacher: mobile ya desktop?

Section 1 kehta hai teacher "strictly desktop". Section 3 ka title "Mobile + Web Portal" hai aur T-02/T-03 me mobile bar bhi hai. Ek chuno.

Naam ya anonymized ID?

T-02 aur T-04 me student ka anonymized ID hai. Aaj ka queue aur students page student ka naam aur email dikhata hai.

Credits aur paisa

Wallet, IAP, Razorpay, teacher payout — kuch bhi code me nahi. Isme Play Billing aur tax/KYC dono lagenge; ye sabse bada naya hissa hai.

Content taiyar hai

WordMaster 2000 (56 column), 220 Chunks aur 100 Hindi-English frames repo me Excel me hain. Flashcard (S-06) aur exercise (S-07) inhi se bharenge.
