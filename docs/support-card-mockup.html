हिंदी मतलब · Hindi meaning

क्या आप एक पल होल्ड कर सकते हैं? मैं यह आपके लिए देखता/देखती हूँ।

Kya aap ek pal hold kar sakte hain? Main yeh aapke liye dekhta/dekhti hoon.

☺ Tone

"Could you…, please?" is a request — the customer can say no. "Hold on" or "Wait" sounds like an order.

⚠ Watch out

✗ Hold the line, I check it.

✓ I'll check that for you.

After I'll use the base verb: I'll check, I'll see, I'll confirm.

❓ Ask next — when you come back

"Thank you for holding. Is the order number ending in 4821?"

👂 Replies you may hear

"Sure, go ahead." · "How long will it take?" · "Can't you just tell me now?"

✎ Grammar in one line

Could you + base verb = polite request. I'll = a decision made now.

2. Which one is polite?

A) Wait, I am checking.
B) Could you hold for a moment, please?
C) You must hold now.

Correct! B asks; A and C give orders.
